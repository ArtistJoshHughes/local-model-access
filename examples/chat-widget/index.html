Example help centre

A plain page with the <lma-chat> widget in the lower right. The engine here is the stand-in: it checks your model file and proves the wiring, but it does not run the model.

Refunds

Orders can be refunded within 30 days. Open the order, choose Refund, and the money returns to the original payment method within five working days.

Delivery

Standard delivery takes three to five working days. Tracking appears in the order once it ships.

Accounts

You can change your email or password under Settings, Account. Deleting an account removes its order history after 30 days.
